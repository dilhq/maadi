Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Maadi Labs handles information when you use our games and applications.

1. Information We Collect

Our games generally do not require an account or login. We do not require your name, email address, phone number, or other personal information to play.

Depending on the game and the services integrated into it, certain information may be collected automatically, including device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, app usage, gameplay, diagnostics, and crash information.

2. Advertising

Some of our games may contain advertisements. Third-party advertising providers may collect information from your device to deliver, measure, and improve advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

3. Offline Gameplay

Our games may be playable without an internet connection. Features such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, online features, and updates may require an internet connection.

4. Local Game Data

Game progress, settings, preferences, unlocks, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device. Removing the game or clearing its data may delete this information.

5. Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, app distribution, infrastructure, and other operational purposes. The services used may vary between games and are governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly require children to provide personal information to access our core gameplay. Third-party services used in our games may have additional requirements concerning children's data and advertising.

7. Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through our games. Third-party services may retain information according to their own policies. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices & Rights

Depending on your device, location, and applicable law, you may have choices or rights regarding personalized advertising, tracking, privacy permissions, access, correction, deletion, or restriction of personal information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always show the updated date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact Maadi Labs at privacy@maadilabs.com.
